From "I want a funded degree" to "I accepted a full offer"

gradcode artifacts. These pages are the approved design for the repo EhsanulHaqueSiam/gradcode, kept in its docs/mocks folder: Main spec · Journey (this page) · Pipeline, Vault and Writer · Plan and status.

v1 finds professors and money. A student needs more: the search has to know who they are and where they're from, it has to read every source (not only LinkedIn), it has to ask when no funding clue exists, follow up more than once, and stay with them through applications, interviews and offers until full funding is secured. This page maps that journey, the features it needs, and the treg endpoints that power each one.

1Who the student is, and why it changes the search

TrackWhat they needWhat changes in the search
Direct PhDAn advisor with money, a program that funds every yearAdvisor money matters most. Professor outreach is the application in advisor-funded systems; in committee systems (top US CS) it's "apply and name me".
MS + PhDA master's that converts to a funded PhD, or a PhD that awards an MS on the wayProgram structure matters: funded from year 1 or only after qualifying exams. Europe often requires a master's before the PhD, so this track opens Europe.
Funded master'sTuition plus a living stipend for the whole programAdvisors matter less, scholarships matter more: government and program schemes, many of them tied to the student's citizenship.

Home country is a hard filter, not a detail

What it decidesExamples
Who may be paidUS NIH T32 training grants and the NSF GRFP fund US citizens and permanent residents only, so an international admit needs a department guarantee or a PI's grant. Some UK studentships cap international places.
Which scholarships existFulbright (US, run by country commissions), Chevening (UK master's), Commonwealth, Erasmus Mundus (EU joint master's, any nationality), DAAD EPOS (Germany, development-related courses), MEXT (Japan), CSC (China), GKS (Korea).
Tests and waiversWhether a medium-of-instruction certificate replaces IELTS or TOEFL depends on the program and the student's country; so do application-fee waivers.
Degree fitA 3-year bachelor's may not qualify for a US PhD or a European master's; a 4-year one may skip the master's in some systems.
Visa and moneyProof-of-funds rules, visa steps and timelines, and whether a stipend covers rent for someone with dependents.

2The journey, end to end

Profilewho you are, citizenship, tests, papers
Targettracks, places, fields, funding floor
Discoverprofessors and programs from every source
Vet moneygrants, guarantees, hiring signals
Reach outpersonal email, sent from your account
Follow up2 to 3 touches, stop on reply
Applydeadlines, fees, SOPs, recommenders
Offerinterviews, compare, negotiate, accept

The hunt ends when a fully funded offer is accepted, not when emails are sent. Every stage after "Vet money" is new work.

How it's gathered

  • A CV (PDF) read by Claude, confirmed fact by fact (built).
  • Links: personal site, Scholar, GitHub, ORCID, LinkedIn. Each is fetched and cross-checked, not trusted alone.
  • A short interview for what a CV never says: citizenship, test plans, dependents, fee budget. Asked once, in setup.
  • Gaps become questions in the sidebar (the Input status), never guesses.

5When there's no clear funding clue

TierEvidenceWhat gradcode does
1 · clearA posted funded opening, or "I have funding for 1 to 2 students in Fall 2027"Top of the list; outreach in cover-letter mode, citing the post
2 · strongAn active grant as PI that lasts past the intake; a new hire's startup package (first 1 to 3 years); a program that funds every admitEmail, naming what you'd work on under that grant
3 · indirectLab growing (new students on the page), recent industry gifts or awards, senior students graduating, steady student-led papersEmail that asks, politely, whether they're taking funded students
4 · none foundNothing public either wayDon't drop them: a short "are you taking students?" email is the cheapest evidence there is. Their reply becomes the record.

Fit and money are scored separately and shown as two numbers, so a perfect-fit professor with tier-4 money gets an asking email instead of silence.

6Outreach: mail, then follow up until someone answers

Sending

  • Drafts come from confirmed facts plus one or two of the professor's recent papers; lint blocks any claim the profile lacks.
  • Approve one, a day, or all; schedule for 08:00 in the professor's timezone, Tuesday to Thursday.
  • Warm-up for a new mailbox: 5 a day in week 1, 10 in week 2, then 15; at most 2 a day per university.
  • Sent from the student's own mailbox. treg has no send endpoint, so: connect Gmail (send and read), or IMAP/SMTP with an app password, or "open in my mail app" for people who connect nothing.
  • Plain text, one recipient, 1 to 2 links, no tracking pixels.

Follow-ups and replies

TouchWhenSays
Firstday 0Who you are, one fit fact, one question
Follow-up 1+7 business daysShort bump, a new angle (their latest paper)
Follow-up 2+14 business daysLast note, offers a CV or a call
After applyingon submit"I applied and named you", with the application ID

Stops on any reply, bounce, out-of-office with a return date (re-timed), or "not taking students". Never follows up apply-only professors. Replies are classified (interested, call, apply first, not taking, needs info) and an answer is drafted within hours.

7After outreach: stay until the money is real

StageWhat the student needs
ApplicationsOne row per program: deadline countdown, fee and waiver, required tests, documents, portal status, and which professors to name. A tailored statement of purpose per program from confirmed facts.
RecommendersWho writes which letter, gentle reminders before each deadline, a thank-you note after.
InterviewsA prep pack per professor (their recent papers, likely questions), calendar entry, a thank-you email.
OffersCompare stipend against local rent, tuition coverage, guaranteed years, insurance, TA load; draft a polite negotiation; track response deadlines (April 15 in the US).
Accept and moveVisa steps for the destination country, documents, dates. The hunt settles here.
