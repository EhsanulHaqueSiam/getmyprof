Outreach like a sales pipeline, and an hq inside the app

getmyprof artifacts. These pages are the approved design for the repo EhsanulHaqueSiam/getmyprof, kept in its docs/mocks folder: Main spec · Journey · Pipeline, Vault and Writer (this page) · Plan and status.

Every professor becomes a deal that moves from to contact to funded. Mail and LinkedIn land in one inbox, sorted by whose turn it is, with follow-ups that run on their own and stop when someone answers. Behind it sits a vault: every fact about you with its proof, every document, every scholarship and program. The agent writes your SOP, CV and emails from it, citing the fact behind each claim.
